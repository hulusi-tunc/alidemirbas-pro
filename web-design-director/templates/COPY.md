## Status per section

Each section below is marked one of:

- **APPROVED** — final, verbatim, safe to compose against.
- **WORKING** — real draft, may still change; composition may proceed but
  should flag anything that depends heavily on exact wording.
- **PLACEHOLDER** — explicitly marked as such (anti-patterns #9's lifecycle
  rule: legal during exploration, a blocking defect at ship). Never
  presented as final without requalifying it.
- **MISSING** — named as missing, with what's needed to fill it. This is a
  legitimate, complete answer for this field — silence is not.
