## What to learn from the corpus
Use the corpus to calibrate:
- terminology actually used by growth, CRM, lifecycle, CRO, SEO, performance and product-marketing practitioners;
- how concepts are named in Turkish versus English;
- level of specificity;
- information hierarchy;
- concise headings, labels and UI copy;
- journey/flow vocabulary.

Do **not** copy sentences or mimic one brand's distinctive voice. Do not assume every sentence on these sites is good copy. Prefer recurring, useful conventions across the corpus and reject generic marketing filler.

## Source roles
- CXL: CRO, experimentation, growth and practitioner terminology.
- Midas: contemporary Turkish digital-product language and concise UI/product explanation.
- Insider: personalization, lifecycle, journey, segmentation and martech terminology.
- Related Digital: Turkish CRM, omnichannel, segmentation and journey terminology.
- Braze: lifecycle, customer engagement, orchestration and channel terminology.
- Klaviyo: flows, triggers, email/SMS/push and commerce lifecycle terminology.
- Zeo: Turkish SEO, performance and digital-marketing terminology.
- HubSpot: CRM, funnel, lead, conversion and marketing terminology.

## Existing site
