## DOC-214 — Document Delivery

- Canonical ID: `DOC-214`
- Slug: `document-distribution`
- Display name: Document Delivery
- Canonical name (chain form): Document distribution → send or provide access → confirm or fail
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/document-distribution`
- Category: Documents, records, signatures & versioned artifacts
- Goal: delivery-confirmation
- Channels: email
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Get a specific issued version to the party who should have it, without either fact touching the other.

> Get a specific issued version to the party who should have it, without either fact touching the other.

### Trigger
- Event: `issued_document_requires_distribution`
- Meaning: an issued document version with a party who is to receive it
- Not enough on its own: a document drafted but not issued; a document already viewed or downloaded by its recipient, which is delivery having happened
- Requires: an issued document version with a party who is to receive it
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the issued version, the intended recipient, and the distribution of one to the other
- instance: document_version_id + recipient_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- an issued document version with a party who is to receive it
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the issued version
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] A document being issued is not the recipient having received it.
- [canonical rule] Sending a wrong or outdated version is prevented by binding distribution to a version.
- [canonical rule] Distribution reuses the canonical communication mechanics rather than duplicating them.

### Recommended orchestration · single-notice
1. **distribute** (transactional, mandatory, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - Channel roles: persistent
   - Purpose: Raise the actual delivery through the canonical communication mechanism, which owns channels, permissions, retries and delivery evidence.

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [success] the identified version reached the intended recipient; the document's validity is unchanged by this (re-entry: a superseding version requires its own distribution. Nothing about this one is retracted by that - the recipient was correctly given what was current then)
- [handoff] external:external-status-reconciliation — a distribution whose outcome could not be established
- [handoff] Message Delivery Recovery — a failed document distribution

### Configure
- `document_distribution.distribution` [config required] (external-window): configure document_distribution.distribution — The distribution outcome is waited for as long as the communication mechanism's own delivery window runs; past it the outcome is reconciled, never assumed.
- `document_distribution.touches` [recommended default]: 1 — Every touch runs against a budget fixed when the instance opened; the budget is the plan's own length, and no touch is repeated because nothing could tell whether it arrived.
- `document_distribution.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — This journey is per the issued version; a later instance concerns a different the issued version and no cooldown applies between them.

### Reusable rule
Document distribution delivers a specific issued version to an intended recipient without changing the document's underlying validity or status.

### Entity (notes)
- the issued version, the intended recipient, and the distribution of one to the other
- The distribution is bound to a version. Delivery state describes the distribution and never the document.

### Guardrails
1. A document being issued is not the recipient having received it.
2. Sending a wrong or outdated version is prevented by binding distribution to a version.
3. Distribution reuses the canonical communication mechanics rather than duplicating them.

### Technical logic (graph, 11 nodes)
- **t.requires** [trigger] (entry, evidence: authoritative)
  - Event id: `issued_document_requires_distribution`
  - Issued document requires distribution
  - evidence: authoritative
  - requires: an issued document version with a party who is to receive it
  - not enough on its own: a document drafted but not issued
  - not enough on its own: a document already viewed or downloaded by its recipient, which is delivery having happened
  - -> a.recipient (node)
- **a.recipient** [action]
  - Resolve the intended recipient and the route permitted for a document of this type. A document reaching the wrong party is worse than one not sent - the second can be retried and the first cannot be recalled
  - writes document_log (append)
  - -> a.version (node)
- **a.version** [action]
  - Bind the distribution to the exact issued version. Sending an outdated version is the failure this step exists to prevent: the recipient then holds, relies on, and may sign terms that nobody currently offers
  - writes document_log (append)
  - -> a.distribute (node)
- **a.distribute** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Raise the actual delivery through the canonical communication mechanism, which owns channels, permissions, retries and delivery evidence. Document distribution does not build its own delivery path - a parallel one will disagree with the canonical delivery record about whether the contract was ever sent
  - writes document_log (append)
  - -> w.distribution (node)
- **w.distribution** [wait]
  - until distribution is confirmed to the required level, or distribution fails
  - Detail: timeout after The distribution outcome is waited for as long as the communication mechanism's own delivery window runs; past it the outcome is reconciled, never assumed. (configure document_distribution.distribution)
  - a distribution neither confirmed nor failed leaves nobody able to say whether the recipient has the document, which is the one thing distribution exists to establish
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.outcome [on event] (node)
  - -> a.unknown [on timeout] (node)
- **c.outcome** [condition] (2 branches)
  - How did the distribution resolve?
  - -> a.confirmed [Confirmed]: delivery or availability is established to the level the requirement demands (node)
  - -> h.recover [Failed]: the channel authoritatively reports it did not arrive (node)
- **a.unknown** [action]
  - Record the distribution outcome as unknown and do not treat it as delivered. The document's validity is untouched either way - what is unknown is whether anybody has it
  - writes document_log (append)
  - -> h.reconcile (node)
- **a.confirmed** [action]
  - Record DELIVERED or AVAILABLE against the exact version distributed. Delivery state describes the distribution and never the document - an undelivered contract is a valid contract nobody has, and treating delivery as validity turns a mail failure into a legal one
  - writes document_log (append)
  - -> x.distributed (node)
- **h.recover** [handoff]
  - Delivery failure → classify → retry, fallback or stop
  - Detail: a failed document distribution
  - carries: message_id (this distribution's own message identity) and destination_id (the recipient destination this attempt targeted), which CMS-208's own recovery instance is keyed on
  - carries: the failure as the channel reported it, and the exact version that was being sent
  - carries: the explicit requirement that any fallback route carries the same version - a recovery that sends a different one is worse than the original failure
  - -> CMS-208 (journey)
- **h.reconcile** [handoff] (external)
  - external:external-status-reconciliation
  - Detail: a distribution whose outcome could not be established
  - carries: the version, the recipient and everything last known about the delivery
  - carries: the explicit fact that the document remains valid and issued regardless of how this resolves
  - -> external:external-status-reconciliation (external)
- **x.distributed** [exit]
  - the identified version reached the intended recipient; the document's validity is unchanged by this
  - Detail: a superseding version requires its own distribution. Nothing about this one is retracted by that - the recipient was correctly given what was current then
