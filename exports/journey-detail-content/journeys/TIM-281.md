## TIM-281 — Expired Access Recovery

- Canonical ID: `TIM-281`
- Slug: `expired-entity-re-entry`

- Canonical name (chain form): Expired entitlement touched → re-entry route → requalify, replace or stay expired
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/expired-entity-re-entry`

- Channels: email, in-app

**Purpose**: Give somebody who has come back to something that expired the one route that actually restores it, at the moment they are asking - because naming the wrong route spends the only intent this ever gets.

### Reusable rule
Somebody returning to something expired has already done the hard part, and the only way to waste that is to name the wrong way back.

### Entity (notes)
- the expired entity and the holder's attempt to act on it, kept distinct from any replacement issued
- The expired entity and its replacement are two objects. That something was valid until March and that something is valid now are different facts, and merging them destroys both.

### Distinct from
- TIM-69 (Expired state → re-entry eligibility → renew, requalify or remain expired) — TIM-69 decides which mechanism restores validity and issues any replacement. This journey starts from the holder's own attempt and carries them along whichever route that decision names.
- TIM-63 (Expiry approaching → eligibility check → renew, complete or let expire) — TIM-63 runs before expiry, while the thing is still valid and can simply be kept. This runs after, where a gap exists on the record and the route back is a different mechanism.

### Guardrails
1. The route is worked out before anything is said. One wrong route spends an intent that arrived willing.
2. An expiry is never undone by editing its date. The gap during which nothing was valid stays on the record.
3. The replacement and the expired entity stay separately addressable, and the holder is told which one they now have.
4. A dead end is said once, clearly, with whatever alternative exists - not softened into a route that does not work.

- **c.route** [condition] (4 branches)

  - -> a.replace [Replacement only]: the expired entity cannot become valid again and a new one has to be issued in its place (node)

- **a.renew** [action] (execution: communication)
