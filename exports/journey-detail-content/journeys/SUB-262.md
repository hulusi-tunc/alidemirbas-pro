## SUB-262 — Cancellation Confirmation

- Canonical ID: `SUB-262`
- Slug: `cancellation-wind-down-notice`
- Display name: Cancellation Confirmation
- Canonical name (chain form): Cancellation confirmed → wind-down window → access ends or customer returns
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/cancellation-wind-down-notice`
- Category: Subscriptions, contracts, renewals & continuing relationships
- Goal: cancellation-termination
- Channels: email, in-app
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Carry somebody through the period between deciding to leave and actually losing access, so the end date is never a surprise and returning stays possible right up to it.

> Carry somebody through the period between deciding to leave and actually losing access, so the end date is never a surprise and returning stays possible right up to it.

### Trigger
- Event: `cancellation_confirmed`
- Meaning: an authoritative cancellation recorded against the relationship; an effective end date
- Not enough on its own: a stated intention to cancel; a failed payment; a support conversation about leaving
- Requires: an authoritative cancellation recorded against the relationship; an effective end date
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the cancelled relationship plus its effective end date
- instance: relationship_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- an authoritative cancellation recorded against the relationship
- an effective end date
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the cancelled relationship plus its effective end date
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] The cancellation is never re-litigated. A save attempt after the decision is a different journey and belongs before this one.
- [canonical rule] Paid-for access runs to its end date. Cancelling early does not shorten it.
- [canonical rule] The end date is stated in the first message and never moves silently.
- [canonical rule] Withdrawal exits the journey immediately - a reminder that access is ending, sent to somebody who has just stayed, is worse than sending nothing.

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- in-session (in-app) — the person is active in the product and the action is taken there
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [invalid-state] cancellation withdrawn, relationship continues (re-entry: a later cancellation starts a new wind-down)
- [success] ended with nothing outstanding (re-entry: a former customer returning enters through acquisition or reactivation, not here)
- [handoff] Continuing Relationship End Reconciliation — a cancelled relationship with obligations that continue past its end

- `cancellation_wind.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — This journey is per the cancelled relationship plus its effective end date; a later instance concerns a different the cancelled relationship plus its effective end date and no cooldown applies between them.

### Reusable rule
The period between deciding to leave and leaving is still a relationship, and it is the one where being told the truth matters most.

### Entity (notes)
- the cancelled relationship plus its effective end date
- The wind-down belongs to this cancellation. A second cancellation after a reactivation is a new instance with its own window.

### Distinct from
- RET-28 (Cancellation intent → understand state → save or proceed) — RET-28 runs before the decision and may offer an alternative. This starts once cancellation is confirmed, and never re-litigates it.
- SUB-167 (Cancellation request → determine effective end → schedule or cancel now) — SUB-167 establishes whether and when the relationship ends. This is what the customer is told across that window.

### Guardrails
1. The cancellation is never re-litigated. A save attempt after the decision is a different journey and belongs before this one.
2. Paid-for access runs to its end date. Cancelling early does not shorten it.
3. The end date is stated in the first message and never moves silently.
4. Withdrawal exits the journey immediately - a reminder that access is ending, sent to somebody who has just stayed, is worse than sending nothing.
