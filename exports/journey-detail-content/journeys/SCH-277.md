## SCH-277 — Booking Confirmation

**Purpose**: Tell the requester whether the specific time they asked for is now a commitment, and where it is not, offer the nearest time that actually exists - because the availability they were shown earlier was a picture and never a hold.

### Reusable rule
Availability shown is a picture and a confirmation is a promise, and the requester must never have to guess which one they were sent.

### Entity (notes)
- the reservation request, the requester, and the resource and slot it names
- One instance per request. A retried submission is the same request rather than a second claim on the same capacity.

### Distinct from
- SCH-173 (Reservation request → validate → confirm, reject or pending) — SCH-173 revalidates capacity and decides whether a commitment exists. This journey carries that outcome to the requester and never states a confirmation the booking record does not hold.

- **c.outcome** [condition] (3 branches)
  - What did revalidation decide?

  - -> a.reoffer [Gone, but something near]: the slot was no longer available when it was re-read, and current availability holds something close enough to be worth offering (node)
  - -> a.decline [Gone, nothing near]: the slot was no longer available and nothing in current availability is a genuine alternative (node)
- **a.lapse** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Close the request as lapsed and say that nothing is held and nothing was booked. Requesters read silence as confirmation, which is the most expensive assumption in scheduling
  - -> x.lapsed (node)
- **a.confirm** [action] (execution: communication)
  - State the committed slot, the resource and the terms concretely - the date, the time, the place, what is needed on arrival. A confirmation that does not restate the specifics is not something the requester can act on a month later
  - -> x.confirmed (node)
- **a.reoffer** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Say the requested time is gone, name the slots available now, and give a deadline for choosing. What is offered is current availability and never the set they were originally shown, which by definition contains one slot that no longer exists
  - -> w.choice (node)
- **a.decline** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Say plainly that the time could not be committed, that nothing is being held, and when capacity of this kind is next expected. A rejection with no next horizon sends the requester somewhere else rather than back to the calendar
  - -> x.declined (node)
- **x.lapsed** [exit]

  - Detail: a fresh request starts the sequence again
- **x.confirmed** [exit]

  - Detail: a change or cancellation to this commitment is its own instance; a further request is a new one
- **w.choice** [wait]

  - Detail: timeout after The stated deadline for choosing. (configure reservation_outcome.choice)
  - the offered slots stay visible to everyone else and are not held while one requester decides
  - engagement does not extend the window

- **x.declined** [exit]

- **c.choice** [condition] (2 branches)
