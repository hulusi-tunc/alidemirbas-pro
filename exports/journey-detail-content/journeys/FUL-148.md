## FUL-148 — Failed Delivery Recovery

- Canonical ID: `FUL-148`
- Slug: `delivery-attempt-failure`
- Display name: Failed Delivery Recovery
- Canonical name (chain form): Delivery attempt failed → reason → retry, correct, alternate or return
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/delivery-attempt-failure`
- Category: Orders, fulfillment, delivery & service completion
- Goal: recovery-retry
- Channels: email, sms
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Recover a failed delivery according to why it failed, within a bounded number of attempts.

> Recover a failed delivery according to why it failed, within a bounded number of attempts.

### Trigger
- Event: `authoritative_delivery_attempt_failure`
- Meaning: a delivery executor confirming an attempt was made and delivery did not occur
- Not enough on its own: a tracking status that has not advanced, which is silence rather than a failure
- Requires: a delivery executor confirming an attempt was made and delivery did not occur
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the individual delivery attempt and the obligation it was serving
- instance: delivery_attempt_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- a delivery executor confirming an attempt was made and delivery did not occur
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the individual delivery attempt and the obligation it was serving
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] Attempted is not delivered.
- [canonical rule] Delivery is not retried indefinitely - the budget is bounded and does not reset.
- [canonical rule] Refused and unavailable are different outcomes with different consequences.
- [canonical rule] The failure reason is never invented. What the executor reported is what is acted on.

### Recommended orchestration · notice-then-confirm
1. **correct** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: What kind of failure was it?
   - Channel roles: persistent -> urgent
   - Purpose: Request the exact correction - the address, the access instruction, the contact.
   - destination: `delivery-correction` · bound to `delivery_attempt_id`
2. **offer-route** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: What kind of failure was it? · Is a reattempt available within the bounded policy? · Would an alternative route serve better than another attempt at the same one?
   - Channel roles: persistent -> urgent
   - Purpose: Put the concrete alternatives in front of the recipient - the collection point, the different window, the other executor - and ask which they want, stating that the attempt budget does not reset either way.
   - destination: `delivery-alternatives` · bound to `delivery_attempt_id` · must not claim: an alternative that is not actually available

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- urgent (sms) — an asserted time bound lies inside the urgent horizon and permission for messages on this channel is recorded
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [handoff] Delivery Outcome Tracking — a further delivery attempt being dispatched
- [handoff] Post-Purchase Issue Recovery — a delivery that cannot be completed - refused, uncorrectable, or out of attempts
- [handoff] Fulfillment Exception Recovery — an item that arrived damaged

### Configure
- `delivery_attempt.correction` [config required] (response-window): configure delivery_attempt.correction — The correction is waited for as long as a reattempt inside the bounded policy is still possible; past it the delivery goes to return.
- `delivery_attempt.route_choice` [config required] (response-window): configure delivery_attempt.route_choice — The choice of alternative is waited for as long as a reattempt inside the bounded policy is still possible; an unanswered choice reattempts the same route.
- `delivery_attempt.touches` [recommended default]: 2 — Every touch runs against a budget fixed when the instance opened; the budget is the plan's own length, and no touch is repeated because nothing could tell whether it arrived.
- `delivery_attempt.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — This journey is per the individual delivery attempt and the obligation it was serving; a later instance concerns a different the individual delivery attempt and the obligation it was serving and no cooldown applies between them.

### Reusable rule
Failed delivery attempts should recover according to the reason delivery failed while preserving the original fulfillment obligation until its terminal outcome is known.

### Entity (notes)
- the individual delivery attempt and the obligation it was serving
- The obligation survives every failed attempt. What changes is how many attempts remain and what would make the next one work.

### Guardrails
1. Attempted is not delivered.
2. Delivery is not retried indefinitely - the budget is bounded and does not reset.
3. Refused and unavailable are different outcomes with different consequences.
4. The failure reason is never invented. What the executor reported is what is acted on.

### Technical logic (graph, 15 nodes)
- **t.failed** [trigger] (entry, evidence: authoritative)
  - Event id: `authoritative_delivery_attempt_failure`
  - Authoritative delivery attempt failure
  - evidence: authoritative
  - requires: a delivery executor confirming an attempt was made and delivery did not occur
  - not enough on its own: a tracking status that has not advanced, which is silence rather than a failure
  - -> a.classify (node)
- **a.classify** [action]
  - Classify the failure into the class the executor actually reported. Refused and unavailable are different outcomes - one is a decision by the recipient and the other is an absence, and treating the first as the second keeps redelivering to someone who has already said no
  - writes delivery_log (append)
  - -> c.class (node)
- **c.class** [condition] (5 branches)
  - What kind of failure was it?
  - -> a.correct [Correctable information needed]: the destination is wrong, or access to it is blocked in a way information would resolve (node)
  - -> c.budget [A reattempt is safe]: the recipient was unavailable, the time window was missed, or the executor itself failed (node)
  - -> h.return [The recipient refused it]: someone with authority to refuse did so (node)
  - -> h.exception [Damaged]: what arrived is not what should have been delivered (node)
  - -> c.budget [No usable reason given]: the executor reported a failure that cannot be turned into an action (node)
- **a.correct** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Request the exact correction - the address, the access instruction, the contact. Naming what is missing is what makes it fixable, and a generic notice that delivery failed sends the recipient to guess
  - writes delivery_log (append)
  - -> w.correction (node)
- **c.budget** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Is a reattempt available within the bounded policy?
  - -> c.alternate [Attempts remain]: the policy's attempt limit has not been reached (node)
  - -> h.return [Exhausted]: the attempt limit is reached (node)
- **h.return** [handoff]
  - Post-completion issue → validate → remedy route
  - Detail: a delivery that cannot be completed - refused, uncorrectable, or out of attempts
  - carries: the failure classification and the full attempt history
  - carries: the obligation, which is unresolved rather than discharged - and any financial consequence, which is a separate lifecycle - obligation_id stands in for REM-151's order_id
  - carries: a fresh issue_id, minted at this handoff and deterministically derived from delivery_attempt_id - FUL-148 has no issue concept of its own, so REM-151's instance is opened here rather than carried
  - -> REM-151 (journey)
- **h.exception** [handoff]
  - Fulfillment exception → diagnose → recover, substitute or fail
  - Detail: an item that arrived damaged
  - carries: what was damaged and the scope it affects
  - carries: the fact that this is a fulfillment problem rather than a delivery one - the delivery worked and the thing delivered did not
  - -> FUL-145 (journey)
- **w.correction** [wait]
  - until the recipient provides the requested correction
  - Detail: timeout after The correction is waited for as long as a reattempt inside the bounded policy is still possible; past it the delivery goes to return. (configure delivery_attempt.correction)
  - an item held indefinitely awaiting information nobody is providing is one that has to go somewhere, and returning it is the honest ending
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.budget [on event] (node, back-edge)
  - -> h.return [on timeout] (node, back-edge)
- **c.alternate** [condition] (3 branches)
  - Would an alternative route serve better than another attempt at the same one?
  - -> a.alternate [An alternative is better, and policy authorises it]: a collection point, a different window or another executor is more likely to succeed, and policy permits the change without asking (node)
  - -> a.offer-route [An alternative is better, but it is the recipient's to choose]: the change would move where or when they must be present, which policy does not let us decide for them (node)
  - -> a.reattempt [Reattempt the same route]: the original route remains the best option (node)
- **a.alternate** [action]
  - Use the authorised alternative route, recorded as a change of route rather than a new obligation. Reached either because policy permits the change or because the recipient chose it - the authority exists before the route moves
  - writes delivery_log (append)
  - -> h.retry (node)
- **a.offer-route** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Put the concrete alternatives in front of the recipient - the collection point, the different window, the other executor - and ask which they want, stating that the attempt budget does not reset either way. Moving where somebody has to be, without asking, is a decision taken on their behalf
  - -> w.route-choice (node)
- **a.reattempt** [action]
  - Schedule the bounded reattempt, against the remaining attempt budget rather than a fresh one
  - writes delivery_log (append)
  - -> h.retry (node)
- **h.retry** [handoff]
  - Dispatch or handoff → track → delivered, failed or unknown
  - Detail: a further delivery attempt being dispatched
  - carries: the attempt history and the remaining budget, which does not reset
  - carries: whatever correction or route change was applied
  - -> FUL-147 (journey)
- **w.route-choice** [wait]
  - until the recipient selects an alternative route, or the recipient declines every alternative
  - Detail: timeout after The choice of alternative is waited for as long as a reattempt inside the bounded policy is still possible; an unanswered choice reattempts the same route. (configure delivery_attempt.route_choice)
  - an offer of a slot or a collection point stops being true once it is gone, and holding the obligation open against a stale offer is worse than reattempting the route we already have
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.route-answer [on event] (node)
  - -> a.reattempt [on timeout] (node, back-edge)
- **c.route-answer** [condition] (2 branches)
  - What did the recipient say?
  - -> a.alternate [Selected an alternative]: they named one of the offered routes (node, back-edge)
  - -> h.return [Declined all of them]: none of the offered routes works for them and they said so (node, back-edge)
