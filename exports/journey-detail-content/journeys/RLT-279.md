## RLT-279 — Upgrade Blocker Reminder

- Canonical ID: `RLT-279`
- Slug: `upgrade-blocker-prompt`
- Display name: Upgrade Blocker Reminder
- Canonical name (chain form): Upgrade blocked by a resolvable prerequisite → prompt → ready or expired
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/upgrade-blocker-prompt`
- Category: Deployment, rollout, change & version transitions
- Goal: readiness-revalidation
- Channels: in-app, email
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Tell the holder of a blocked target the one specific thing standing between it and the change, while there is still enough of the preparation window left for them to clear it.

> Tell the holder of a blocked target the one specific thing standing between it and the change, while there is still enough of the preparation window left for them to clear it.

### Trigger
- Event: `target_held_on_named_prerequisite`
- Meaning: a target authoritatively held for a planned change; the specific prerequisite that is blocking it, named rather than described; a preparation window with time still in it
- Not enough on its own: a change that is available but not yet scoped to this target; a hold whose cause has not been identified
- Requires: a target authoritatively held for a planned change; the specific prerequisite that is blocking it, named rather than described; a preparation window with time still in it
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the target, the change waiting on it, and the named prerequisite that is blocking it
- instance: target_id + change_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- a target authoritatively held for a planned change
- the specific prerequisite that is blocking it, named rather than described
- a preparation window with time still in it
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the target
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] The prompt names the specific blocker. A notice that an update is available, sent to somebody who cannot take it, is the failure this journey exists to stop.
- [canonical rule] Nothing is asked of a holder who cannot clear the prerequisite.
- [canonical rule] Two prompts at most, and both name the same blocker and the same date.
- [canonical rule] Not ready is not failed. A target that was never touched is recorded as unprepared, not as a failed change.

### Recommended orchestration · offer-decide-remind
1. **inform-hold** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Can the holder clear this blocker themselves?
   - Channel roles: in-session -> persistent
   - Purpose: Say that the change is held and why, with nothing asked of the holder - because there is nothing they can do.
2. **name-blocker** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Can the holder clear this blocker themselves?
   - Channel roles: in-session -> persistent
   - Purpose: Name the one prerequisite, what clearing it involves, and the date after which the change can no longer be applied in this window.
   - destination: `clear-the-prerequisite` · bound to `target_id` · must not claim: a moved window
3. **last-call** (service, canonical rule)
   - Timing: The point in the preparation window past which a target still blocked cannot be made ready in time. (configure upgrade_blocker.clear) (relative to preparation_window_ends_at)
   - cancelled by the specific named requirement that blocked progress is recorded as satisfied; the change request is withdrawn
   - re-read before sending: the the target re-read from the system of record before acting on the timeout
   - checks: Is a second prompt still worth sending?
   - Channel roles: in-session -> persistent
   - Purpose: Send one further prompt naming the same prerequisite and the date it stops mattering.
   - destination: `clear-the-prerequisite` · bound to `target_id` · must not claim: a moved window
   - after t2

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- in-session (in-app) — the person is active in the product and the action is taken there
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [success] held on a blocker the holder cannot clear (re-entry: if the blocker later becomes theirs to clear, the target qualifies again on the resolvable path)
- [invalid-state] change withdrawn before the blocker was cleared (re-entry: the next change with the same prerequisite scopes this target again)
- [timeout] preparation window closed with the blocker outstanding (re-entry: the target stays an unprepared member of the population and is prompted again by the next change that needs the same prerequisite)
- [handoff] Change Readiness — a named prerequisite cleared by the holder inside the preparation window

### Configure
- `upgrade_blocker.clear` [config required] (attribute-bound): configure upgrade_blocker.clear — The point in the preparation window past which a target still blocked cannot be made ready in time.
- `upgrade_blocker.final` [config required] (attribute-bound): configure upgrade_blocker.final — After the last call the instance waits until the preparation window itself closes; there is no third prompt.
- `upgrade_blocker.touches` [recommended default]: 2 — Every touch runs against a budget fixed when the instance opened; the budget is the plan's own length, and no touch is repeated because nothing could tell whether it arrived.
- `upgrade_blocker.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — This journey is per the target; a later instance concerns a different the target and no cooldown applies between them.

### Reusable rule
A blocked change is worth telling somebody about only when the blocker is theirs to clear and the window is still open enough for clearing it to matter.

### Entity (notes)
- the target, the change waiting on it, and the named prerequisite that is blocking it
- One target, one blocker, one change. A second blocker on the same target is named in the same prompt or not at all - two prompts about one target read as two problems.

### Distinct from
- RLT-242 (Change preparation → resolve dependencies → ready or hold) — RLT-242 works out what preparation this change requires and records the blockers that hold the target. This journey is what the holder is told about a blocker, and it sends nothing until that hold exists with a named cause.
- RLT-241 (Change candidate → eligibility → ready or blocked) — RLT-241 decides whether the target is in scope for the change at all. Here scope is already settled and the only open question is a prerequisite.

### Guardrails
1. The prompt names the specific blocker. A notice that an update is available, sent to somebody who cannot take it, is the failure this journey exists to stop.
2. Nothing is asked of a holder who cannot clear the prerequisite.
3. Two prompts at most, and both name the same blocker and the same date.
4. Not ready is not failed. A target that was never touched is recorded as unprepared, not as a failed change.
