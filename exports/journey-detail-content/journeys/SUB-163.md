## SUB-163 — Renewal Reminder

- Canonical ID: `SUB-163`
- Slug: `renewal-decision`
- Display name: Renewal Reminder
- Canonical name (chain form): Renewal window → eligibility → renew, non-renew or review
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/renewal-decision`
- Category: Subscriptions, contracts, renewals & continuing relationships
- Goal: eligibility-qualification
- Channels: email, in-app, push, sms
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Reach a decision about the next term, as a decision - separate from anything that makes the next term real.

> Bring a renewal cycle to a recorded decision before the notice deadline: give the notice the terms require, put the decision to whoever holds it where one is needed, and apply what the terms say when none is made.

### Trigger
- Event: `renewal_decision_window_opens`
- Meaning: a relationship whose term end is inside the renewal decision window its governing terms define
- Not enough on its own: a renewal reminder having been sent, which is a communication and not a decision window opening
- Requires: a relationship whose term end is inside the renewal decision window its governing terms define
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the continuing relationship and the renewal cycle currently open on it
- instance: relationship_id + renewal_cycle_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- a renewal decision window has opened on a continuing relationship with a current term
- the renewal terms, model and required notice are defined - or the cycle goes to decision resolution first
- no renewal instance is already open for this cycle
- no open payment recovery process exists on the relationship - payment recovery owns the relationship until it resolves

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] A window with no defined notice period or renewing terms goes to decision resolution (DEC-181); nothing is asked or noticed on undefined terms.
- [canonical rule] An outstanding blocker puts the cycle in review; the relationship stays active on its current term throughout and no decision is requested until the blocker is settled.
- [canonical rule] A recorded, authorised decision ends asking; nothing further is sent by this journey once the decision exists.
- [canonical rule] Putting the decision to its holder is not deciding; a non-response is resolved by what the governing terms define, never assumed.
- [canonical rule] An open payment recovery process on the relationship suppresses the renewal decision request entirely; a routine renewal ask is not put to someone whose current term is already in question over an unresolved payment failure.
- [canonical rule] Hard gates (GLB-31) apply; pressure caps do not to the required notice, which is an obligation of the terms rather than outreach.

### Recommended orchestration · deadline-countdown
1. **required-notice** (transactional, mandatory, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Are the renewal terms and required notice period defined? · Do the governing terms require notice to actually be given?
   - Channel roles: persistent -> in-session
   - Purpose: Give the notice the terms require: the renewal model that will apply, the terms it renews on, and what happens if no decision is made.
   - why this priority: a notice the governing terms require is an obligation of the relationship, not outreach; it is never rationed or deferred
   - destination: `renewal-terms` · bound to `renewal_cycle_id`
2. **decision-request** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Does an outstanding blocker prevent the renewal from being decided? · What does the renewal model require?
   - Channel roles: persistent -> in-session -> urgent
   - Purpose: Put the renewal decision to whoever holds it, with the terms that would apply and the point by which the notice period requires an answer.
   - destination: `renewal-decision` · bound to `renewal_cycle_id` · must not claim: that a decision has been made

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the notice and the terms must be kept, and are addressed to whoever holds the decision - the default for a renewal
- in-session (in-app) — the decision holder is active in the product and the decision is taken there
- urgent (sms, push) — the notice deadline is inside the urgent horizon and permission for service messages on the channel is recorded
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [suppression] renewal decision suppressed; a cancellation in motion on the relationship takes precedence (re-entry: the cancellation's own resolution decides what happens next - if it is withdrawn, the renewal cycle re-opens fresh rather than resuming a decision made under a since-lifted cancellation)
- [handoff] Decision Request — a renewal window with no defined notice period or renewing terms
- [handoff] Ownership Escalation — a renewal review outliving the notice period
- [handoff] Renewal Execution — a renewal decided and ready for execution
- [handoff] Scheduled Cancellation Revalidation — a non-renewal scheduling an end at the current term's end

### Configure
- `renewal.decision_deadline` [recommended default] (attribute-bound): term_end_at minus the notice period the terms require — The decision is waited for until the last point at which the required notice period still allows one; then the governing terms decide.
- `renewal.notice_deadline` [recommended default] (attribute-bound): term_end_at minus the notice period the terms require — A review that outlives the notice deadline is escalated to ownership; the relationship stays on its current term meanwhile.
- `renewal.discretionary_touches` [recommended default]: 1 — Only the decision request counts against the cap; the notice the terms require is an obligation, not a touch to ration.
- `renewal.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — Renewal is per cycle; the next cycle is its own instance and no cooldown applies between cycles.

### Required data
**Semantic events to map**
- `renewal_decision_window_opens` (authoritative) — a relationship whose term end is inside the renewal decision window its governing terms define
- `renewal_decision_recorded` (authoritative) — an authorised renewal decision is recorded
**Attributes**: relationship_id · renewal_cycle_id · term_end_at · renewal_model · notice_period · renewing_terms · decision_holder
**optional**: blockers · has_active_session · urgent_channel_permission

### Collision & priority
- priority: service · mandatory touches: t-notice
- pressure class: service
- local cap: 1 (non-mandatory)
- cooldown: none
- competition: relationship-continuity (subscription) - below a cancellation in motion, below an active risk state, and below an open payment recovery process on the same relationship
- No action when s.undefined-terms · s.blocked · s.decided · s.asking-not-deciding · s.payment-recovery · s.hard-gates — the suppressions above are recorded outcomes, never a fallback to another channel

### Measurement
- journey outcome: handoff: h.execute, h.scheduled-end, h.escalate, h.undefined
- business outcome: renewal_decision_recorded — an authorised renewal decision is recorded
- observed: in this journey
- attribution: entered-before-event · not-applicable
- guardrails: complaint · notice_missed · decision_assumed · support_contact_within_24h

### Reusable rule
Renewal is a new term decision governed by the current relationship state and applicable renewal rules.

### Entity (notes)
- the continuing relationship and the renewal cycle currently open on it
- One renewal cycle per term boundary. A relationship renewed eight times has eight cycles in its history, each with what was decided and why.

### Distinct from
- SUB-164 (Renewal execution → financial and dependency check → new term active) — This produces a decision. SUB-164 makes the new term exist, which depends on payment, confirmation and eligibility that this journey does not touch. A relationship can be decided-to-renew and still not renew.

### Guardrails
1. Renewal eligible is not renewed.
2. A renewal communication is not a renewal decision.
3. Renewal notice periods and renewing terms are never invented.
4. A relationship under renewal review stays active on its current term.
5. A relationship with a cancellation already in motion, or with an active risk state, is not sent a routine renewal message. The lifecycle that already owns the person takes precedence, and a renewal reminder arriving during a cancellation reads as a system that is not paying attention.
6. A cancellation that starts in motion after the decision request was already sent is not resolved as a renewal or a non-renewal - w.decision's and w.review's own recheck surface it, and c.decision's own branch defers to it (x.superseded) rather than letting a stale wait resolve to a decision the relationship no longer stands behind.

### Technical logic (graph, 20 nodes)
- **t.window** [trigger] (entry, evidence: authoritative)
  - Event id: `renewal_decision_window_opens`
  - Renewal decision window opens
  - evidence: authoritative
  - requires: a relationship whose term end is inside the renewal decision window its governing terms define
  - not enough on its own: a renewal reminder having been sent, which is a communication and not a decision window opening
  - -> a.evaluate (node)
- **a.evaluate** [action]
  - Evaluate renewal eligibility, the renewal model, the relationship's current state, the notice the terms require, the pricing and terms that would apply, any outstanding blockers, and whether the counterparty has to decide at all
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> c.notice (node)
- **c.notice** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Are the renewal terms and required notice period defined?
  - -> c.notice-required [Defined]: the governing terms state the notice period, the renewal model and the terms that would apply (node)
  - -> h.undefined [Not defined]: the notice period or the renewing terms are not stated anywhere authoritative (node)
- **c.notice-required** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Do the governing terms require notice to actually be given?
  - -> a.notice [Notice is required]: the terms oblige us to tell them the term is renewing before it does (node)
  - -> c.blockers [No notice obligation]: the terms define the model and the period but require no notification (node)
- **h.undefined** [handoff]
  - Decision request → validate → route, reject or hold
  - Detail: a renewal window with no defined notice period or renewing terms
  - carries: the relationship, its term end and what the terms do say
  - carries: the explicit fact that no notice period or price was invented in order to proceed
  - -> DEC-181 (journey)
- **a.notice** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Give the notice the terms require: the renewal model that will apply, the terms it renews on, and what happens if they do nothing. Having defined a notice period is not the same as having given notice, and an auto-renew that reaches execution silently is exactly the case the obligation exists for. This is notice, not a request - it is never treated as the decision
  - -> c.blockers (node)
- **c.blockers** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Does an outstanding blocker prevent the renewal from being decided?
  - -> a.review [Blocked]: an unresolved obligation, dispute or eligibility problem stands in the way (node)
  - -> c.model [Clear]: nothing outstanding prevents a decision (node)
- **a.review** [action]
  - Record RENEWAL_REVIEW with what has to be settled. The relationship stays active on its current term throughout - a renewal under review is not a relationship in trouble
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> w.review (node)
- **c.model** [condition] (3 branches)
  - What does the renewal model require?
  - -> a.decided [Auto-renew, requirements met]: the terms renew automatically and every condition for that is satisfied (node)
  - -> a.request [An explicit decision]: the terms require the counterparty to choose (node)
  - -> a.review [Review first]: the terms require an internal decision before renewal can be offered (node, back-edge)
- **w.review** [wait]
  - until an authorised renewal decision is recorded
  - Detail: timeout after A review that outlives the notice deadline is escalated to ownership; the relationship stays on its current term meanwhile. (recommended: term_end_at minus the notice period the terms require; configure renewal.notice_deadline)
  - a review that outlives the notice period has removed the counterparty's ability to plan, whichever way it eventually goes
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.decision [on event] (node)
  - -> h.escalate [on timeout] (node)
- **a.decided** [action]
  - Record the renewal as decided, with the new term's dates and the terms that would apply. Decided is not renewed - the new term does not exist until its own requirements have been met, and a relationship can sit here and still lapse
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> h.execute (node)
- **a.request** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Put the renewal decision to whoever holds it, with the terms that would apply. Asking is not deciding - a renewal notice sent is a communication, and treating the send as the answer renews relationships nobody agreed to
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> w.decision (node)
- **c.decision** [condition] (3 branches)
  - What is the outcome for the next term?
  - -> x.superseded [Cancellation in motion]: the cycle re-read shows a cancellation now in motion on the relationship - this journey's own declared precedence is below an active cancellation, and resolving a renewal decision independently while one is in motion would contradict it (node)
  - -> a.decided [Renew]: the decision, or the terms' default, is to continue, and no cancellation is in motion (node, back-edge)
  - -> a.non-renew [Do not renew]: the decision, or the terms' default, is to let the term end, and no cancellation is in motion (node)
- **h.escalate** [handoff]
  - Responsibility escalation → higher authority → resolution or return
  - Detail: a renewal review outliving the notice period
  - carries: the relationship, its term end and what the review is waiting on
  - -> OWN-55 (journey)
- **h.execute** [handoff]
  - Renewal execution → financial and dependency check → new term active
  - Detail: a renewal decided and ready for execution
  - carries: renewal_cycle_id, the decision, the new term's dates and its terms
  - carries: the explicit fact that the new term does not yet exist and its requirements have not been tested
  - -> SUB-164 (journey)
- **w.decision** [wait]
  - until an authorised renewal decision is recorded
  - Detail: timeout after The decision is waited for until the last point at which the required notice period still allows one; then the governing terms decide. (recommended: term_end_at minus the notice period the terms require; configure renewal.decision_deadline)
  - the notice period is what makes the deadline real - past it, the terms themselves determine what happens, and pretending the decision is still open misrepresents the relationship
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.decision [on event] (node, back-edge)
  - -> a.default [on timeout] (node)
- **x.superseded** [exit]
  - renewal decision suppressed; a cancellation in motion on the relationship takes precedence
  - Detail: the cancellation's own resolution decides what happens next - if it is withdrawn, the renewal cycle re-opens fresh rather than resuming a decision made under a since-lifted cancellation
- **a.non-renew** [action]
  - Record NON_RENEWING with the effective end being the current term's end. The relationship is still active and still governed by its current term - non-renewing is a decision about the next term and says nothing about this one
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> h.scheduled-end (node)
- **a.default** [action]
  - Apply what the governing terms define as the outcome when no decision is made - which for some renewal models is renewal and for others is non-renewal. Record that no decision was made rather than recording a decision, because someone who did not answer did not agree
  - writes renewal_log (append)
  - -> c.decision (node, back-edge)
- **h.scheduled-end** [handoff]
  - Scheduled cancellation → revalidate at effective time → end or preserve
  - Detail: a non-renewal scheduling an end at the current term's end
  - carries: the effective end date and the relationship version the decision was made against
  - carries: the explicit fact that a later renewal, resubscription or plan change supersedes this and must suppress it
  - -> SUB-168 (journey)
