## ACQ-285 — New Lead Welcome

- Canonical ID: `ACQ-285`
- Slug: `captured-interest-first-touch`
- Display name: New Lead Welcome
- Canonical name (chain form): Captured interest → readiness check → destination-appropriate first touch
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/captured-interest-first-touch`
- Category: Acquisition, intent & qualification
- Goal: routing-assignment
- Channels: email
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Answer a declared interest with the thing that interest actually asked for, and carry it onward only as far as what the person said about themselves justifies.

> Answer a declared interest with the thing that interest actually asked for, and carry it onward only as far as what the person said about themselves justifies.

### Trigger
- Event: `first_party_interest_captured`
- Meaning: a first-party submission: a form, a content request, a contact request, or an equivalent deliberate act
- Not enough on its own: a session or a page view with no declaration in it; a contact point obtained from a third party; a submitted form read as permission because it was submitted
- Requires: a first-party capture recorded with the context the person declared; a contact point the person gave in that capture; the permission position recorded as its own fact, separate from the submission
- Source: declared

### Entity
- Scope: the individual capture and what it asked for, resolved onto a person
- instance: capture_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- a first-party capture recorded with the context the person declared
- a contact point the person gave in that capture
- the permission position recorded as its own fact, separate from the submission
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the individual capture and what it asked for
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] A capture records interest, not a decision. Nothing downstream treats it as one.
- [canonical rule] The submission and the permission are two facts, and only one of them may have happened.
- [canonical rule] Not every capture earns a person. The destination comes from what was declared, not from what the pipeline is short of.
- [canonical rule] A bounded sequence states its end when it opens and stops there, whether or not anything came of it.

### Recommended orchestration · notice-then-confirm
1. **first-touch** (promotional, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Is the destination they supplied actually usable for this? · What did the person actually declare?
   - Channel roles: persistent
   - Purpose: Send what they asked for and say that a person will follow up, naming when.
2. **deliver** (promotional, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Is the destination they supplied actually usable for this? · What did the person actually declare?
   - Channel roles: persistent
   - Purpose: Send exactly what the capture asked for, once, and nothing the person did not ask for alongside it.
3. **nurture** (promotional, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Is the destination they supplied actually usable for this? · What did the person actually declare? · Is there permission to continue past fulfilment?
   - Channel roles: persistent
   - Purpose: Open a bounded sequence on the subject they declared, and state where it ends when it opens.
   - destination: `declared-subject-content` · bound to `capture_id` · must not claim: a sequence with no stated end
   - after t2

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [failure] captured with nothing deliverable; the request was not fulfilled (re-entry: a valid permitted destination, supplied later, makes the same request fulfillable)
- [suppression] stopped at the person's request (re-entry: a later capture carrying its own permission is a new instance; this one is never resumed)
- [timeout] sequence ended, no destination declared (re-entry: a later capture from the same person is its own instance with its own destination)
- [success] fulfilled, no continuing contact permitted (re-entry: a later capture that carries a permission opens the bounded path)
- [handoff] external:sales-assignment — a captured interest whose declared request can only be answered by a person

### Configure
- `captured_interest.nurture` [config required] (observation-window): configure captured_interest.nurture — The stated length of the bounded sequence.
- `captured_interest.touches` [recommended default]: 3 — Every touch runs against a budget fixed when the instance opened; the budget is the plan's own length, and no touch is repeated because nothing could tell whether it arrived.
- `captured_interest.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — Fulfilment is per capture; a further capture is its own instance and no cooldown applies between captures.

### Reusable rule
What somebody asked for is the entire mandate for the first message, and everything after it needs a permission of its own.

### Entity (notes)
- the individual capture and what it asked for, resolved onto a person
- One capture, one destination. A second, different request from the same person is its own instance and is not considered answered by what the first one produced.

### Distinct from
- ACQ-02 (Captured interest → qualification → appropriate destination) — ACQ-02 decides which destination a capture justifies and records that decision with its own state. This journey is what the person receives once that decision exists, and it invents no destination of its own.

### Guardrails
1. A capture records interest, not a decision. Nothing downstream treats it as one.
2. The submission and the permission are two facts, and only one of them may have happened.
3. Not every capture earns a person. The destination comes from what was declared, not from what the pipeline is short of.
4. A bounded sequence states its end when it opens and stops there, whether or not anything came of it.

### Technical logic (graph, 15 nodes)
- **t.captured** [trigger] (entry, evidence: declared)
  - Event id: `first_party_interest_captured`
  - First party interest captured
  - evidence: declared
  - requires: a first-party capture recorded with the context the person declared
  - requires: a contact point the person gave in that capture
  - requires: the permission position recorded as its own fact, separate from the submission
  - not enough on its own: a session or a page view with no declaration in it
  - not enough on its own: a contact point obtained from a third party
  - not enough on its own: a submitted form read as permission because it was submitted
  - -> c.email-route (node)
- **c.email-route** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Is the destination they supplied actually usable for this?
  - -> c.declared [Usable]: the supplied destination is valid, deliverable and permitted for fulfilling what was asked for (node)
  - -> c.declared-no-route [Not usable]: no valid deliverable destination was supplied, or it is not permitted for this fulfilment (node)
- **c.declared** [condition] (2 branches)
  - What did the person actually declare?
  - -> a.first-touch [Asked for a person]: the capture states a request only a person can answer - a conversation, a price for their own situation, an assessment (node)
  - -> a.deliver [Asked for the material]: the capture asks for a document, an access or a notification and names no person-led request (node)
- **c.declared-no-route** [condition] (2 branches)
  - What did they ask for, given we cannot deliver to them?
  - -> h.person [Asked for a person]: the request was for contact rather than for material (node)
  - -> x.no-delivery-route [Asked for the material]: the request was for something we would have sent, and there is nowhere to send it (node)
- **a.first-touch** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Send what they asked for and say that a person will follow up, naming when. A promised follow-up with no time on it reads as a queue rather than an answer, and the person starts again elsewhere
  - -> h.person (node)
- **a.deliver** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Send exactly what the capture asked for, once, and nothing the person did not ask for alongside it. Fulfilment travels on the request; everything past it travels on permission, and the two must not be posted together
  - -> c.permission (node)
- **h.person** [handoff] (external)
  - external:sales-assignment
  - Detail: a captured interest whose declared request can only be answered by a person
  - carries: what the person declared and asked for, in their own terms
  - carries: what was already sent to them and when
  - carries: the permission facts recorded, and what they do and do not cover
  - -> external:sales-assignment (external)
- **x.no-delivery-route** [exit]
  - captured with nothing deliverable; the request was not fulfilled
  - Detail: a valid permitted destination, supplied later, makes the same request fulfillable
- **c.permission** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Is there permission to continue past fulfilment?
  - -> a.nurture [Permitted]: a permission covering ongoing contact was given in the capture and recorded as its own fact (node)
  - -> x.delivered [Fulfilment only]: nothing beyond the single requested delivery was permitted (node)
- **a.nurture** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Open a bounded sequence on the subject they declared, and state where it ends when it opens. A sequence with no stated end is a subscription nobody agreed to, and it is remembered as one
  - -> w.nurture (node)
- **x.delivered** [exit]
  - fulfilled, no continuing contact permitted
  - Detail: a later capture that carries a permission opens the bounded path
- **w.nurture** [wait]
  - until the person does something that names where they are going: a reply, a request to talk, a purchase, or the person withdraws the permission this communication runs on
  - Detail: timeout after The stated length of the bounded sequence. (configure captured_interest.nurture)
  - the end was stated when the sequence opened, and moving it silently is what turns interest into complaint
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.progressed [on event] (node)
  - -> x.sunset [on timeout] (node)
- **c.progressed** [condition] (2 branches)
  - What ended the sequence?
  - -> h.person [Readiness declared]: the person did something that names a destination they are now ready for (node, back-edge)
  - -> x.stopped [Stopped]: the person withdrew permission or asked to stop (node)
- **x.sunset** [exit]
  - sequence ended, no destination declared
  - Detail: a later capture from the same person is its own instance with its own destination
- **x.stopped** [exit] (terminal)
  - stopped at the person's request
  - Detail: a later capture carrying its own permission is a new instance; this one is never resumed
