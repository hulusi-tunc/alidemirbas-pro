## DEC-184 — More Information Request

- Canonical ID: `DEC-184`
- Slug: `information-requirement`
- Display name: More Information Request
- Canonical name (chain form): More information required → collect → revalidate → resume review
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/information-requirement`
- Category: Approvals, decisions, reviews & human-in-the-loop work
- Goal: recovery-retry
- Channels: email, task
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Pause a decision for the fact it is actually missing, without losing the review already done.

> Pause a decision for the fact it is actually missing, without losing the review already done.

### Trigger
- Event: `information_requirement_identified`
- Meaning: a reviewer identifying a specific fact the decision turns on and which is not available
- Not enough on its own: a reviewer's curiosity about something the decision does not turn on; information missing before the case was opened, which DEC-181 holds as pending information
- Requires: a reviewer identifying a specific fact the decision turns on and which is not available
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the decision case and the specific information requirement blocking it
- instance: decision_case_id + requirement_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- a reviewer identifying a specific fact the decision turns on and which is not available
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the decision case and the specific information requirement blocking it
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] More information required is not rejected.
- [canonical rule] The review history is never reset when information arrives.
- [canonical rule] Unrelated information is not requested alongside the requirement.
- [canonical rule] Deadline behaviour comes from policy rather than from a default closure.

### Recommended orchestration · notice-then-confirm
1. **request** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Who actually holds the outstanding information?
   - Channel roles: persistent
   - Purpose: Request what is still outstanding from the external source, naming it precisely.
   - destination: `provide-outstanding-information` · bound to `requirement_id` · must not claim: a moved deadline, that the case is rejected
2. **request-internal** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Who actually holds the outstanding information?
   - Channel roles: human
   - Purpose: Raise the outstanding requirement as owned work against the internal holder, carrying the blocked decision and its unchanged deadline.

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- human (task) — the step is carried out by a person - a call, a task, a visit - and recorded as done by them
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [invalid-state] no longer requiring a decision; the review history stands unclosed rather than concluded (re-entry: a renewed need for the same judgment is a new request, which can reference this case and its evidence)
- [timeout] closed for want of information; no decision was made on the merits (re-entry: a new request with the information present is assessed fresh, and this closure does not count against it)
- [handoff] Decision Escalation — an information deadline expiring with escalation or with no defined closure semantics
- [handoff] Evidence Review — an information requirement resolved, or exhausted under policy

### Reusable rule
Missing information pauses the decision while preserving the existing case so review can resume when the defined evidence requirement is satisfied.

### Entity (notes)
- the decision case and the specific information requirement blocking it
- The requirement is the evidence this decision turns on. It is not a general data-completeness exercise, and nothing unrelated is collected alongside it.

### Distinct from
- FBK-49 (Missing critical data → request or resolve → resume) — FBK-49 resolves missing data blocking a process generally. This is evidence a named reviewer needs to answer a specific open question - it is scoped by the decision rather than by the record, and it ends by resuming a review rather than by unblocking a pipeline.

### Guardrails
1. More information required is not rejected.
2. The review history is never reset when information arrives.
3. Unrelated information is not requested alongside the requirement.
4. Deadline behaviour comes from policy rather than from a default closure.

### Technical logic (graph, 19 nodes)
- **t.identified** [trigger] (entry, evidence: authoritative)
  - Event id: `information_requirement_identified`
  - Information requirement identified
  - evidence: authoritative
  - requires: a reviewer identifying a specific fact the decision turns on and which is not available
  - not enough on its own: a reviewer's curiosity about something the decision does not turn on
  - not enough on its own: information missing before the case was opened, which DEC-181 holds as pending information
  - -> a.specify (node)
- **a.specify** [action]
  - Specify the exact requirement - which fact, which document, from whom, and why the decision needs it. A vague request produces a vague answer and a second round; and asking for unrelated things in case they help turns one gap into a questionnaire, which is how a two-day case becomes a three-week one
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> a.state (node)
- **a.state** [action]
  - Record AWAITING_INFORMATION and preserve everything the review has already established. The case is paused rather than reset - more information required is not a rejection, and a reviewer returning to it should not be starting again from the beginning
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> c.source-type (node)
- **c.source-type** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Who actually holds the outstanding information?
  - -> a.request [The requester or another external party]: only someone outside the organisation can supply it (node)
  - -> a.request-internal [Someone inside the organisation]: an internal owner, team or system holds it, or is the one authorised to produce it (node)
- **a.request** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Request what is still outstanding from the external source, naming it precisely. Re-requesting the whole requirement asks for things already given, which reads as the first submission having been ignored - and asking a customer for something an internal system already holds is the most common version of this failure
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> w.info (node)
- **a.request-internal** [action] (execution: human)
  - Raise the outstanding requirement as owned work against the internal holder, carrying the blocked decision and its unchanged deadline. An internal evidence gap routed down a customer channel asks the wrong party on a route they never agreed to
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> w.info (node)
- **w.info** [wait]
  - until the requested information is received, or the requester withdraws the request
  - Detail: timeout after The deadline policy defines for this requirement. (configure information_requirement.info)
  - a case awaiting information indefinitely is a decision nobody will ever make, and the requester is left believing it is progressing
  - engagement does not extend the window
  - -> c.received [on event] (node)
  - -> a.deadline [on timeout] (node)
- **c.received** [condition] (2 branches)
  - How did the wait resolve?
  - -> a.validate [Information arrived]: something was supplied against the requirement (node)
  - -> a.moot [The decision is no longer required]: the request was withdrawn or the thing it was about no longer exists (node)
- **a.deadline** [action]
  - Apply the timeout semantics policy defines - escalation, closure, or a decision on the evidence available. Which one applies comes from policy: a case that quietly closes because nobody answered is a rejection nobody made, and a case that quietly proceeds is a decision made on evidence somebody knew was incomplete
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> c.timeout (node)
- **a.validate** [action]
  - Validate what arrived for relevance and completeness against the stated requirement. Something arriving is not the requirement being met - a document of the right type saying the wrong thing satisfies a checklist and not a decision
  - -> c.complete (node)
- **a.moot** [action]
  - Record the case as no longer requiring a decision, with the reason. This is not a rejection and not an approval, and reporting it as either tells the requester something untrue about what happened
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> x.moot (node)
- **c.timeout** [condition] (3 branches)
  - What does policy define for an expired information deadline?
  - -> h.resume [Decide on what is available]: policy permits deciding on the evidence in hand, with the gap recorded as a gap (node)
  - -> a.close [Close the case]: policy explicitly closes an unanswered case, with defined semantics for doing so (node)
  - -> h.escalate [Escalate, or policy does not define it]: policy escalates - or says nothing, in which case no closure semantics are invented here (node)
- **c.complete** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Does what arrived satisfy the requirement?
  - -> h.resume [It does]: the requirement is met, relevantly and completely (node)
  - -> c.further [Partly]: some of the requirement is met and some is not (node)
- **x.moot** [exit]
  - no longer requiring a decision; the review history stands unclosed rather than concluded
  - Detail: a renewed need for the same judgment is a new request, which can reference this case and its evidence
- **h.resume** [handoff]
  - Review started → evaluate evidence → decide or request more information
  - Detail: an information requirement resolved, or exhausted under policy
  - carries: what was supplied and what remains missing, stated as a gap rather than smoothed over
  - carries: the full prior review history, so the reviewer continues rather than restarts
  - -> DEC-183 (journey)
- **a.close** [action]
  - Close the case under the semantics policy actually states, recording that it closed for want of information rather than on its merits
  - writes decision_log (append)
  - -> x.closed-noinfo (node)
- **h.escalate** [handoff]
  - Decision escalation → higher authority → decide, return or reassign
  - Detail: an information deadline expiring with escalation or with no defined closure semantics
  - carries: the requirement, what was and was not supplied, and the review already done
  - carries: the explicit fact that no closure or rejection was invented to end the case
  - -> DEC-189 (journey)
- **c.further** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Does policy allow requesting the remainder?
  - -> c.source-type [It does, within the request budget]: further rounds are permitted and the case has not used them all (node, back-edge)
  - -> h.resume [It does not, or the budget is exhausted]: no further round is permitted, or the case has already been round-tripped as often as the model allows (node, back-edge)
- **x.closed-noinfo** [exit]
  - closed for want of information; no decision was made on the merits
  - Detail: a new request with the information present is assessed fresh, and this closure does not count against it
