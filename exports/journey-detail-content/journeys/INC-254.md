## INC-254 — Incident Update

- Canonical ID: `INC-254`
- Slug: `incident-communication`
- Display name: Incident Update
- Canonical name (chain form): Incident communication → identify affected cohort → inform, update or close
- Source route: `/lab/customer-journeys/incident-communication`
- Category: Incidents, service disruption, operations & recovery
- Goal: delivery-confirmation
- Channels: email, in-app
- Surface: customer (communicating)

**Purpose**: Tell the people actually affected something true and useful, through the mechanism that already owns delivery.

> Tell the people actually affected something true and useful, through the mechanism that already owns delivery.

### Trigger
- Event: `incident_reaches_communication_relevant_state`
- Meaning: an incident state change that could change what an affected party should do or expect
- Not enough on its own: time having passed since the last update, which is not itself information
- Requires: an incident state change that could change what an affected party should do or expect
- Source: authoritative

### Entity
- Scope: the incident, the affected recipient cohort and the communication obligation it creates
- instance: incident_id + recipient_cohort_id + state_change_id · one-active-per-key

### Who enters
- an incident state change that could change what an affected party should do or expect
- no instance of this journey is already open for the the incident
- hard gates (GLB-31) allow communication for this purpose

### Suppressed when
- [canonical rule] No root cause or resolution is claimed before it is confirmed.
- [canonical rule] No promotional content appears in incident communication.
- [canonical rule] Uncertain technical detail is never communicated as fact.
- [canonical rule] The affected cohort is identified as precisely as the evidence allows.

### Recommended orchestration · single-notice
1. **communicate** (service, canonical rule)
   - sent on classification - no wait before it
   - checks: Can the affected cohort be identified with reasonable precision? · Is what would be said actually confirmed? · Does this materially change the guidance the recipient already has?
   - Channel roles: persistent -> in-session
   - Purpose: Raise the communication through the canonical communication mechanism, which owns the obligation, the recipient resolution, the channels, the permissions and the delivery evidence.

**Channel roles** (recommended default)
- persistent (email) — the message has to be kept and survive until the person can act on it
- in-session (in-app) — the person is active in the product and the action is taken there
- delivery fallback: same-role-other-channel

### Stops when
- [no-action] no update sent; nothing material had changed (re-entry: the next material change, or the next point a commitment requires an update, brings this back)
- [success] update issued; the next update is bound to a stated condition rather than to a clock (re-entry: the stated condition occurring, or a material change, produces the next update)
- [success] resolution communicated to this recipient scope (re-entry: a relapse is communicated as a relapse rather than as a new incident, because the recipients were told it was over)

### Configure
- `incident_communication.touches` [recommended default]: 1 — Every touch runs against a budget fixed when the instance opened; the budget is the plan's own length, and no touch is repeated because nothing could tell whether it arrived.
- `incident_communication.cooldown` [recommended default]: none — This journey is per the incident; a later instance concerns a different the incident and no cooldown applies between them.

### Reusable rule
Incident communication should provide verified, actionable information to the population actually affected by the incident.

### Entity (notes)
- the incident, the affected recipient cohort and the communication obligation it creates
- This decides what should be said and to whom. Channels, permissions, delivery and retries belong to the communication lifecycle.

### Guardrails
1. No root cause or resolution is claimed before it is confirmed.
2. No promotional content appears in incident communication.
3. Uncertain technical detail is never communicated as fact.
4. The affected cohort is identified as precisely as the evidence allows.

### Technical logic (graph, 14 nodes)
- **t.relevant** [trigger] (entry, evidence: authoritative)
  - Event id: `incident_reaches_communication_relevant_state`
  - Incident reaches communication relevant state
  - evidence: authoritative
  - requires: an incident state change that could change what an affected party should do or expect
  - not enough on its own: time having passed since the last update, which is not itself information
  - -> a.determine (node)
- **a.determine** [action]
  - Determine who is materially affected, what is known, what is not known, what action the user needs to take, any safe workaround, and the condition under which the next meaningful update would happen. Saying what is not yet known is information; leaving it out and stating the rest as certainty is not
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - -> c.cohort (node)
- **c.cohort** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Can the affected cohort be identified with reasonable precision?
  - -> a.scoped [It can]: the affected population is identifiable by account, region, capability or cohort (node)
  - -> a.broad [It cannot yet]: the boundary of the impact is genuinely unclear (node)
- **a.scoped** [action]
  - Scope the communication to the affected population. Telling everybody about an incident affecting one region trains the whole base to ignore incident notices, and the next one will be one that matters to them
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - -> c.verified (node)
- **a.broad** [action]
  - Broaden only as far as necessary, and say explicitly that the scope is still being established. Uncertainty stated is information; uncertainty implied as precision is a claim that will have to be corrected
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - -> c.verified (node)
- **c.verified** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Is what would be said actually confirmed?
  - -> c.material [Confirmed]: every claim in it is established rather than believed (node)
  - -> a.hold-claim [Not confirmed]: the cause, the fix or the timing is still a hypothesis (node)
- **c.material** [condition] (3 branches)
  - Does this materially change the guidance the recipient already has?
  - -> a.communicate [It does]: what they should do, expect or avoid has changed (node)
  - -> a.no-send [It does not, and no commitment requires an update]: nothing has changed for them since the last message (node)
  - -> a.communicate [It does not, but a commitment requires an update]: a status-page cadence, a contract or a regulatory obligation requires one regardless (node)
- **a.hold-claim** [action]
  - Say what is known and what is not, and claim nothing about cause or resolution that has not been established. A root cause announced and then retracted costs more credibility than a slow update, and technical detail stated as fact while still uncertain is the most common source of that retraction
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - -> c.material (node, back-edge)
- **a.communicate** [action] (execution: communication)
  - Raise the communication through the canonical communication mechanism, which owns the obligation, the recipient resolution, the channels, the permissions and the delivery evidence. Incident communication does not build its own delivery path, and the message carries no promotional content
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - -> c.final (node)
- **a.no-send** [action]
  - Send nothing, and record why. Sending because time has passed produces noise, and noise is what makes the eventual resolution notice go unread by the people who were waiting for it
  - writes incident_log (append)
  - writes suppressed_sends (append)
  - -> x.no-send (node)
- **c.final** [condition] (2 branches)
  - Is this the resolution notice for this recipient scope?
  - -> x.closed-comms [It is]: the incident is resolved for the population this message goes to (node)
  - -> x.updated [It is not]: the incident continues for them (node)
- **x.no-send** [exit]
  - no update sent; nothing material had changed
  - Detail: the next material change, or the next point a commitment requires an update, brings this back
- **x.closed-comms** [exit]
  - resolution communicated to this recipient scope
  - Detail: a relapse is communicated as a relapse rather than as a new incident, because the recipients were told it was over
- **x.updated** [exit]
  - update issued; the next update is bound to a stated condition rather than to a clock
  - Detail: the stated condition occurring, or a material change, produces the next update
